OUR JOURNEY

From Serving the Nation to Serving Humanity.

Founded by an ex-Army officer, Maratha Life Foundation was born not in a boardroom, but on the streets. We witnessed individuals bleeding on the margins of society—abandoned elderly, hungry children, and those left with no hope.

Bringing military discipline to compassionate care, we established a true sanctuary. We believe in grassroots action over polished words.

2006 - First Rescue on the Streets
2010 - 100 patients supported
2012 - 150 patients supported
2014 - 200 patients supported
2018 - Vanchalabai Malhari Lokhande Gurukulam started
2021 - Collaboration with Jivan Jyot Drugs Bank
2024 - Integrated with ISKCON Anna Mitra Foundation
2025 - Sarva Mangal Niketan building construction supported by Sarva Mangal Family Trust
OUR PROGRAMS

Our Ground Work

We operate multiple life-changing programs dedicated to rehabilitating and supporting the vulnerable members of our society.

Elderly Orphanage

Providing shelter, medical care, and emotional support to abandoned senior citizens. A safe home where dignity and care come first.

Orphan Care

Nurturing children with education, love, and a secure future. Building lives with compassion and responsibility.

Rehabilitation Center

Helping individuals rebuild their lives through structured programs, counseling, and continuous support for recovery.

Gurukul

A value-based education system focused on discipline, culture, and holistic development for underprivileged children.

De-Addiction Center

Supporting individuals in overcoming addiction through medical care, therapy, and a strong support system.

Gaushala

Caring for and protecting cows with proper shelter, nutrition, and medical attention as part of our cultural and humanitarian mission.
